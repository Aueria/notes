Turns ratio is defined as:
$$
\frac{n_2}{n_1}
$$
1. Voltage seen on the secondary side is Voltage $\times$ turns ratio:
2. Current seen on the secondary side is Current / turns ratio :
3. Effective impedance seen between the secondary terminals (right hand side of xfmr) is the impedance $\times$ by the square of turns ratio

Regarding impedance:

![[Pasted image 20250729203821.png]]

1. Resistance is the same (multiply by square of turns ratio)
2. Inductance increase proportionally. Therefore, the output is the same.
3. Capacitance has an inverse relationship. Therefore the turns ratio are divided instead.
