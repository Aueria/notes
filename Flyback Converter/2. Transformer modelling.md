It is modelled with the K element
```
K1 L1 L2 K
```
K1 = name of transformer
L1 = Primary inductance
L2 = Secondary inductance
K = coupling coefficient (i.e. 0.999 = 99.9%)

# Coupling coefficient (K)

## Primary Inductance measurement ($L_p$)

![[Pasted image 20250815142345.png]]

## Leakage Inductance measurement ($L_k$)

![[Pasted image 20250815142520.png]]

Coupling coefficient can be then calculated by:
$$
K = \sqrt{1 - \frac{L_k}{L_p}}
$$

The "K model" of LTspice uses a coupled inductor method. Behind the scenes, the circuit actually looks like this:

# Switching ringing

We show the circuit with the coupled inductor $L_k$ and the gate/drain-srouce capacitance of the MOSFET $C_{DS}$
![[Pasted image 20250815143437.png]]
The gate capacitance of the MOSFET and the coupled inductance of the transformer forms a LC circuit, which causes the ringing. The frequency of the ringing can be calculated by:
$$
f_0 = \frac{1}{2 \pi L_k \cdot C_{DS}}
$$
K = 0.99833 ($L_p = 120u, L_k = 400n, C_{DS} = 150p$)
![[Pasted image 20250815144931.png]]

# Snubbing

We can attach a CR circuit parallel to the transformer. General design rules are shown below:

Capacitance: usually 2 to 10 times of $C_{DS}$
Resistance: $Z = \sqrt{\cfrac{L_k}{C_{DS}}}$
