1. In practice, most current mode PWM cannot achieve 50% duty cycle. The UC1844 has maximum duty cycle of 46% (worst case)
2. Most components have power losses. Most flybacks have an efficiency of 70%-90%. An assumption of 80% efficiency is a good estimation

We now re-evaluate our design parameters

1. $V_{in}$ = 24V-34V
2. $V_{out}$ = 12V @ 0.5A
3. Frequency = 100kHz
4. Duty cycle =  0.46
5. Efficiency = 80%

# New calculations 

1. Power Output
$$
P_{out} = 12V \cdot 0.5A = 6W
$$
2. Power Input
$$
\eta = \frac{P_{out}}{P_{in}}
$$
Then
$$
P_{in} = \frac{6W}{0.8} = 7.5W
$$
3. Average Input Current
$$
I_{in,avg} = \frac{P_{in}}{V_{min}} = \frac{7.5W}{24V} = 312mA
$$
4. Input Peak Current
$$
I_{in,pk} = \frac{I_{in,avg}}{0.5 \cdot D} = \frac{0.312A}{0.5 \cdot 0.46} = 1.35A
$$
5. MOSFET on time ($T_{on}$)
$$
T_{on} = \frac{D}{\text{frequency}} = \frac{0.46}{100 \text{kHz}} = 4.6 \mu s
$$
6. Primary inductance
$$
L_p = \frac{V_{min} \cdot T_{on}}{I_{in,pk}} = \frac{24V \cdot 4.6 \mu s}{1.35A} = 81.7 \mu H
$$
